Trying to add friends? Connect with friends like a Pro

To activate the Friender please re-open this window on:

  • Members list of a group Navigate to any Facebook group and click on "Members"
  • Your Suggested friends list Go to Facebook Friends → Find Friends → People You May Know
  • A Friend's Friend list Visit any friend's profile and click on "Friends"
Note: To re-open this tutorial, open this window on a Facebook page with no friends list on it.
